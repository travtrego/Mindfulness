# What your hands did today — eleven minutes

*template: reflective*

Reference session #6. **Reflective** template. No place, no journey, no scenery to lean on.
Attention to what is already here.

This session exists because `reflective` and `immersive` share two role *names* —
`meaningful_experience` and `consolidation` — and the jobs are not the same. In immersive
they mean *movement through a landscape* and *the stillness after it*. Here they mean
**the noticing sequence** and **widening**. An exemplar pulled across templates would teach
the generator to put a landscape in a session that has no landscape.

Depth 1 — one tap, three questions.

**Gratitude is the category most likely to produce garbage.** The failure mode is
abstraction — *be grateful for your health, your loved ones, this moment.* The fix is not
a better tone. It is an object. This session gets one and does not let go of it.

## What makes this template hard

**There is nothing to describe.** Immersive can spend a beat on light and width and
underfoot. Here the entire content is attention, so the noticing language *is* the session
rather than a technique inside it. `craft.py` requires a 12% noticing ratio across a whole
script; this one runs at 0.13 with no scenery to carry any of it.

**Gratitude collapses into abstraction unless it has an object.** Every line above is about
one pair of hands doing one specific thing. The moment a sentence would be equally true of
anyone, it has failed. *"Be grateful for your body"* is true of everyone and lands on no
one.

**No second person's feelings are asserted.** The session never says how the listener feels
about their hands, and never instructs them to feel anything. It only says what the hands
did. The feeling, if it arrives, is theirs and not on the recording.

## `consolidation` here means widening, not stillness

In the immersive template (`docs/sessions/05`) consolidation is where movement stops. Here
there was no movement to stop — the widening *is* the beat. It moves from your hands to the
hands that made the things your hands touched, and then stops, because two steps out is
plenty and three would be a sermon.

> **The widening moves exactly one step.** Hands, then the hands behind the objects. Not to
> humanity, not to the universe. The step must stay small enough to still be concrete.

## The rule this session establishes

> **A reflective session must name an object in its first generated beat and never leave
> it.** If the `object` slot is empty the template cannot run, and the question that fills
> it is not optional.
