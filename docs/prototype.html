Onboarding · asked once

The flow

How many questions before audio

Depth scales with what the template actually needs. Three questions before "just breathing" would be absurd; zero questions before a rehearsal produces a generic run-through. Each category declares its own depth.

DepthPathTapsCategories
0 tap → audio 1 Just breathing · Safe place
1 tap → 3 questions → audio 4 Nature · Adventure · Fantasy · Into sleep · Body scan · Gratitude · Creativity
2 tap → talk → 3 questions → audio ~7 Interview · Competition · Hard conversation · Confidence

Floor: one tap

About two seconds to audio. That's the 2am case, and nothing may be added to it — no confirmation, no "how are you feeling," no length picker.

Ceiling: seven interactions

Roughly 45 seconds. A fair trade for a 14-minute rehearsal that has to know your situation. Not a fair trade for breathing.

Hard caps

3 chat turns. 3 amplifiers. If the model wants a fourth question it doesn't get one — it guesses and moves. Interrogation is a failure state.

Why talk comes first

Because it makes the questions generated rather than pre-authored. "Do they see your face?" only exists because you said "the walk off." No fixed set per category produces that.

Fourteen categories, five templates

Breadth on the surface is cheap. Every category maps to one of five beat structures — so adding "Adventure" beside "Nature" costs one prompt constraint, not a new pipeline.

TemplateShapeCategories
Breath only Settle → pace → return Just breathing
Anchored place Return → deepen → anchor Safe place
Immersive Arrival → movement → stillness Nature · Adventure · Fantasy · Into sleep
Rehearsal Approach → event → aftermath Interview · Competition · Hard conversation · Confidence
Reflective Noticing, non-narrative Body scan · Gratitude · Creativity

What memory does and doesn't carry

Carries

Style. Voice, pacing tolerance, sensory density, preferred length, the kind of detail that lands. Applies whether you're on a ridge or in a tunnel.

Doesn't carry

Content. Tuesday's fantasy never bleeds into Wednesday's soccer game. Emotional topics are opt-in per session and stay in their lane.

Sessions stand alone

Breathing one night, fantasy the next, a match the third — that's normal use, not a failure to engage. Only Safe Place is continuous, because returning to the same place is the point.

Reflections do the teaching

One line at the close beats any amount of skip-and-replay inference. It's language about what landed, available from session one — which is why personalization can ship in V1 after all.
